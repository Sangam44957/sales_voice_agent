# Fees

## Programme fees
Data Science Programme: 60,000 rupees in total. Web Development Programme: 40,000 rupees in total. IELTS Preparation: 15,000 rupees in total.

## Payment options
Fees can be paid in full or in three monthly instalments with no interest. A 10 percent early-bird discount applies if the full fee is paid before the batch starts. Scholarships of up to 20 percent are available for students scoring above 85 percent in the entry test.
