# Admissions

## Admission steps
Step one: book a free counselling session. Step two: take a short entry test, which takes 30 minutes online. Step three: submit ID proof and previous marksheets. Step four: pay the registration fee of 2,000 rupees to confirm the seat.

## Batch dates
New batches start on the first Monday of every month. Seats per batch are limited to thirty students.

## Eligibility
Data Science and Web Development need a high school diploma. IELTS has no minimum eligibility.
