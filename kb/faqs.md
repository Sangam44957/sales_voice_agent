# Frequently asked questions

## Refund policy
A full refund is available within 7 days of the batch start. After that, refunds are not available but fees can be transferred to a later batch once.

## Online or offline
All programmes are available online and at our Jalandhar campus. Recordings are shared for online students.

## Placement support
We provide resume building, mock interviews and referrals to hiring partners. We do not guarantee a job.

## Contact and hours
Counsellors are available Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM. Counselling sessions are free and last 30 minutes.
